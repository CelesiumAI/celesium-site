How it works · The method

AI you can defend.

Defensible is not a feature we added at the end. It is the order the work is done in, and it is why our answers survive the review that follows them.

Every answer comes with its proof

Four parts

A general chatbot hands your team a confident answer and no way to prove it is right. Our platforms make the call and back it. Four things arrive together, every time.

1

The answer.

A direct decision, coded, scored, or recommended, from a platform built for the job rather than a general model guessing.

2

The exact rule.

The statute, regulation, standard, or ruling that governs the call, attached and traceable to source. Not "according to a model." The rule itself.

3

The work shown.

What the platform saw, what it considered, what it ruled out, laid out so a reviewer can audit the reasoning and not just the conclusion.

4

The method disclosed.

How we got there, stated up front, so the result holds up to any auditor, regulator, court, or board.

How a platform gets built.

The same sequence every time, whichever regulated function it serves.

1

Name the decision.

Not a workflow and not a department. One repeated call a qualified person makes today, and has to defend later.

2

Fix the authority.

We identify the controlling rules, versions and effective dates included, and pin them so an answer can point at a specific provision rather than a general impression.

3

Build the path.

Deterministic rules do the work that should be deterministic. The model does the work that genuinely needs judgment, and never the parts that don't.

4

Make it checkable.

Every finding carries its citation and its reasoning. Where the platform is uncertain, it says so and declines rather than filling the gap.

5

Seal the record.

Each analysis is committed to a tamper-evident record, so a later change to the file is visible instead of silent.

6

Keep a human in the loop.

The platform produces a defensible recommendation. A qualified person still owns the decision, which is the only arrangement that survives review.

What we will not do

Boundaries

Answer without a source

If the controlling authority cannot be identified, the platform declines and says why. A confident guess is the failure mode we build against.

Silently correct your data

Anomalies are flagged for human review, never quietly fixed. You see what looked wrong and decide what to do about it.

Train on your work

Client data is analyzed, reported, and left where it belongs. It never enters a shared or public model.

Replace the professional

These platforms are built to make a qualified person faster and better defended, not to remove them from the record.
