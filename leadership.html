The structure.

CelesiumAI LLC is a Texas company headquartered in Houston, incubated under and operated alongside Celesium Capital — a private equity firm with deep roots in healthcare, technology, and real estate.

Form

CelesiumAI LLC

A Texas limited liability company headquartered at 6222 Richmond, Ste 800, Houston, TX 77057. Each portfolio platform is incorporated as a standalone subsidiary or licensed entity.

Parent

Operated under Celesium Capital

CelesiumAI is the technology arm of Celesium Capital — a Houston-based private equity firm focused on healthcare, technology, and real estate. The PE platform provides governance, capital, and institutional relationships.

Where

Houston · Texas Medical Center

We work in proximity to the largest medical complex in the world. Active collaborations with faculty at Baylor College of Medicine, Rice University, MD Anderson, and the Texas Medical Center inform every healthcare and research-security engine in the portfolio.
