— 01 / The stakes

Regulated decisions have to be defensible, not just fast.

The hard part of a decision in healthcare, banking, or compliance was never getting an answer — it was defending that answer when someone asked why. Audit findings, penalties, overturned appeals, sanctions. In your world, an unsourced answer is a liability.
