FIG. D

Every answer comes with its proof.

Generic AI hands your team a confident answer — and no way to prove it's right. Our platforms make the call and back it, in four parts:

1

The answer.

A direct decision — coded, scored, or recommended — from a platform built for the job, not a chatbot guess.

2

The exact rule.

The statute, regulation, or ruling that governs the call — attached, traceable to source. Not "according to a model." The rule itself.

3

The work shown.

What the platform saw, considered, and ruled out — laid out so a reviewer can audit the reasoning, not just the conclusion.

4

The method disclosed.

How we got there, stated up front — so the result holds up to any auditor, regulator, court, or board.
