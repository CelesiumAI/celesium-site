— 01 / The stakes

In your world, the answer has to hold up — not just sound right.

The hard part of a decision in healthcare, banking, or compliance was never getting an answer — it was proving it was right when someone asked why. Audit findings, penalties, overturned appeals, sanctions. An answer with no source behind it is a liability.

— 02 / The difference

Every answer comes with its proof.

Generic AI hands your team a confident answer — and no way to prove it's right. Our AI tools make the call and back it with the exact rule, regulation, or law behind it — so the answer holds up the second it's produced.

— Proof

The answer.

A direct decision — coded, scored, or recommended — produced by an AI tool built for the job, not a generic chatbot guess.

— Proof

The exact rule behind it.

The statute, regulation, standard, or court ruling that governs the call — attached to every answer, traceable to source. Not "according to a model." The rule itself.

— Proof

The work shown.

What the tool saw, what it considered, what it ruled out — laid out so a human reviewer can audit the reasoning, not just the conclusion.

— Proof

How we got there.

The method behind the answer — disclosed up front so the result holds up to any auditor, regulator, court, or board.

How it works in depth
Anyone can give your team an answer. We give you an answer that holds up — to the auditor, the regulator, and the court. That's the difference between AI as a liability and AI as an asset in regulated work.
